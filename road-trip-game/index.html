How it works
  1. Draw, then answer. Whoever's turn it is draws the card, reads it out loud, and answers it first. Then the other guy answers the same one. Every card gets two answers.
  2. Turns alternate. Player 1 draws, then Player 2, all the way down the highway.
  3. Reversal. Three per person per deck. Make the other guy go first on a card you're not ready for.
  4. Tap out. Anything you flat out don't want to answer, tap out and draw a new one. No explanation owed. Use it and it's still a win — you kept playing.
  5. Run-ins. Every so often somebody's music gets hijacked or you owe each other a promo. Both of you do it, and nobody loses a turn over it.
  6. The Last Ride deck is the closer. Save it for the final stretch of the drive.

🛞 Passenger holds the phone and reads the cards. Driver keeps both hands on the wheel and does the talking.
